# Key insights

- Company-wide attrition is 16.1% (237 of 1470 employees left).
- Sales has the highest department attrition at 20.6% (92 of 446); Research & Development is lowest at 13.8%.
- The 0-1 yrs tenure band leaves most often at 34.9% (75 of 215).
- Attrition is 31.3% for employees with the poorest work-life balance (1) versus 14.2% at score 3.
- Human Resources is the highest-risk education field at 25.9% (7 of 27).
- The 'Q1 (lowest 25%)' income band has the highest attrition at 29.3%.
- Attrition is 22.8% at job satisfaction 1 versus 11.3% at satisfaction 4.
- Riskiest segment: Sales with 0-1 yrs tenure, at 39.3% (2.44x the company average).
- People who left earned about 4787 a month on average versus 6833 for those who stayed, and had 5.1 years of tenure versus 7.4.
